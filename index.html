Felix Dülmer

Ph.D. Candidate

I am a Ph.D. candidate in computer science at the Technical University of Munich, supervised by Prof. Nassir Navab at the Chair for Computer Aided Medical Procedures and affiliated with the Institute of Biological and Medical Imaging. My research combines learning-based scene representations with physical models of ultrasound image formation, with a focus on differentiable rendering, novel view synthesis and robot-assisted acquisition.

In 2026, I was a visiting researcher in Prof. Jeremy Dahl's lab at Stanford University, working on differentiable speed-of-sound estimation.
